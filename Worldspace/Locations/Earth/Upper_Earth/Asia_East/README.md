## Significance

Mongolia's, Korea's, and the Sinian Federation's post-war consolidation defines East Asia's political
geography for the remainder of the First Interwar Period, through the founding of the International Court of
Diplomacy at Jeju-Do and its landmark 2318 ruling.

## Settled facts

- The Sinian Federation comprises nine Han states: Zhongyuan, Qin, Bashu, Jin, Wu, Gan, Xiang, Min, and Yue.
  Mongolia, Manchuria, East Turkestan, Tibet, and Taiwan are not members.
- Qinghai is part of Qin — not Tibet, not its own region.
- Qin retained its name after absorbing Qinghai.
- Jeju-do is a three-power condominium (Korea, Japan, the Sinian Federation), home to the International Court
  of Diplomacy. Korean takes precedence in any conflict-in-translation.
- Every polity's native-language name is recorded in `Native_Names.md`.
