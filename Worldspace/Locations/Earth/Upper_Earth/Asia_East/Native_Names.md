## Notes

- "Sinian" traces to Sanskrit *Cīna* via 震旦 (Zhèndàn), a historical Chinese term for China.
- Taiwan uses Traditional Chinese (臺灣); every mainland Han state uses Simplified.
- Manchuria and Inner Mongolia use Mandarin, reflecting their Han-majority populations.
- Unified Korea's native name (한국/Hanguk) derives from dropping the directional prefix shared by 북한
  (Bukhan) and 남한 (Namhan).
- "The Iron Federation" (铁联邦/Tiě Liánbāng) is a colloquial nickname, not the Sinian Federation's official
  name.
