# Ji-Eun Kim — Formative History

Her early life and existence took place during a time when, despite robots having legal personhood rights, global anti-robot sentiment was at an all-time high among humans due to a variety of incidents. She became a nanotech engineer who had to fight tooth and nail to earn respect for her competency and work her way to the top of her field. By the late 25th century, she had become known as one of the greatest and best nanotechnicians on the planet.

Sometime before or during the Long Night War, she began building a piece of personal concealment technology — nanotech countermeasures against detection — as a defensive gift for someone she loved (identity and nature of the relationship not yet established). Before she could complete and deliver it, that person was captured by Upper Earth forces and held at one of their compounds; they did not survive. She had already been building the technology for their protection before this happened — it wasn't a reaction to their death. But their death is why the version she later built for herself, turned inward rather than delivered outward, is permanent and irreversible rather than something she could someday undo.
