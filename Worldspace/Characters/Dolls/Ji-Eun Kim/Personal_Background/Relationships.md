# Ji-Eun Kim — Relationships

## Key Individuals
* Calethina — Connected to Ji-Eun's story and legacy; tracks her whereabouts through the ruins of her testing facility. Does not know Ji-Eun is still alive. The exact nature of their prior connection is not yet established. Not knowing is not a gap in Calethina's own network reach so much as a consequence of her own damaged infrastructure — her distributed server network was itself hit by Amundsen Tower's destruction, the same event that caused the Planetary Split Brain, and her city-wide signal projection only holds intermittently even within Concordia (see Calethina's own README). Ji-Eun's concealment, engineered by Tepenia's foremost detection specialist, would also plausibly account for whatever local signal-grid detection exists in Aquarius specifically, including the moments Calethina's grid does hold.
* Unnamed person (identity not established) — The person Ji-Eun originally built her concealment technology for, as a defensive gift. Captured by Upper Earth forces and held at a compound during the Long Night War era — the same mechanism already documented for other Eyes of Gold-adjacent losses (cf. Favi della Torre's boyfriend) — and did not survive to receive it. Whether this is the same person as that case or a separate one is deliberately left open, pending further development. She carries the technology like an undelivered letter.

## Factions / Groups
* Eyes of Gold — Friend and technical partner, though never a member. She engineered the gold ocular prosthetics that serve as the faction's rite of passage.
