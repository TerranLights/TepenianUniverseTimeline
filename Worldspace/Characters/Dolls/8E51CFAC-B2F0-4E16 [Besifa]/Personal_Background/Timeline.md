  of an interplanetary shipping freighter. **[Sourced]**
* **The freighter's run:** carrying fabrication materials and manufactured goods, plus passengers from Jupiter
  and a handful from Mars taking a cheap voyage to Saturn. **[Sourced]**
  ⭐ **The route is the strongest available lead on her origin and it is sitting in the vision statement.**
