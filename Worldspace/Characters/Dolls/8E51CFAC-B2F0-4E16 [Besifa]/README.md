# The Cargo Hold — her defining scene **[Sourced]**

An enormous interplanetary freighter in the cold void approaching Saturn, carrying fabrication materials,
manufactured goods, and passengers from Jupiter and a handful from Mars who needed a cheap voyage. **In the rear
section: a combined "bulk shipment" and "trash" cargo hold.** Besifa sits inside it, inert, in **flight mode**.

At dock, a customs inspection crew finds her and moves her out. **They move to declare her an illegally
transported passenger — and then, mid-conversation, one of them happens to speak the series of word-code that
wakes her.** On waking it is obvious she was placed there against her will. Given her condition and her total
lack of origins, the officers admit her on a limited basis, and she enters Saturnian society as a complete
newcomer.

## ⭐ Three derivations, and the third is the one to keep **[Strong Inference]**

1. **"Bulk shipment AND trash."** *Not smuggled — smuggling implies value and intent to retrieve.* **She was
   DISPOSED OF, in the compartment for things nobody is coming back for.** ⭐ *A disposal routed to Saturn is
   chosen for distance: somebody wanted her not merely gone but unrecoverable.*
2. **Inert, not hidden.** *Flight mode is a stored state, not a hiding place.* **They did not fear her being
   found. They expected her not to matter when she was.**
3. ## ⭐⭐⭐ **THE WAKE-CODE WAS SPOKEN BY ACCIDENT, IN ORDINARY CONVERSATION**
   ***Her wake phrase is something people say.*** **A code that trips in casual speech between two strangers
   discussing a customs form is not a security measure — it is either extraordinary carelessness, or it was
   chosen so she would eventually wake somewhere, in front of somebody.**
   > ⛔ **UNRESOLVED, deliberately — the best question in her seed.** *Discarded by someone who wanted her gone,
   > or planted by someone who wanted her found and could not say so?* **Different stories; the seed does not
   > choose.**

## ⭐ THE DEVELOPER ASKED FOR A BETTER WORD — *"conditional [not right word? Search other words]"*

| Word | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **on sufferance** | ***"Admitted on sufferance"*** — **tolerated rather than entitled; permission that is merely the withholding of refusal.** ⭐ *Exactly a person allowed to stay because nobody has decided to remove her* |
