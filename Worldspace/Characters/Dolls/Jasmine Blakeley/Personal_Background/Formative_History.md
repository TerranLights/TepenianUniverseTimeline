# Jasmine Blakeley — Formative History

* Built to serve as caretaker of a vineyard winery in the northern-central region of either the Republic of
  Colorado or the Republic of Sonora, New North America — exact nation undecided; the developer needs more time
  to settle this.
* Primary function: maintaining the wine barrels — temperature, duration, and general technical stewardship of
  wine production. Built specifically because the vineyard's owner judged this work a robot would do better,
  and more accurately, than any human.
* Developed a genuine love for beautiful things while doing this work, and came to value wine and wine
  stewardship for its own sake rather than purely as her assigned function.
* Originated a two-technique wine-art practice: a canvas-staining "watercolor" technique using wine of varying
  shades, and a glass-underside-rim "drip" technique that stains a canvas over varying lengths of time — see
  `README.md`'s Backstory Summary for full detail.
* Met a human man — then in his early college-age years — who came to the vineyard to volunteer, tending soil
  and doing most of the physically demanding work. They fell deeply in love; he stayed far longer than he'd
  originally intended; they established a home together once circumstances allowed.
* Timeframe: First Interwar Period, before the Jeju-do robot-personhood ruling (April 27, 2318) — her entire
  established story predates legal robot personhood everywhere.
