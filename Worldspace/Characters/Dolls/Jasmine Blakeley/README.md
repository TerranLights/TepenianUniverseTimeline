## Backstory Summary
- Brief Backstory Summary (abbreviated): Built as a vineyard's wine-barrel caretaker; fell in love with a human
  volunteer; originated a wine-based art practice; the two eventually built a home together.

Jasmine was built to serve as caretaker of a vineyard winery in the northern-central region of either the
Republic of Colorado or the Republic of Sonora (nation undecided). Her primary function was maintaining the wine
barrels — temperature, duration, and the general technical stewardship of production — because her owner judged
this work a robot would do better, and more accurately, than any human. While doing that work, she developed a
genuine love for beautiful things, and came to value wine and wine stewardship for its own sake rather than
purely as her assigned function — which is where her wine-art practice originates.

A human man, then in his early college-age years, came to the vineyard to volunteer, tending soil and doing most
of the physically demanding work. Over time, he and Jasmine fell deeply in love; he stayed far longer than he'd
originally intended to, and once circumstances allowed, they established a home together.

This entire story takes place in the First Interwar Period, before the Jeju-do ruling ever granted robots legal
personhood anywhere — a fact the seed itself doesn't dwell on, but one that's worth keeping in view (see Design
Notes).
