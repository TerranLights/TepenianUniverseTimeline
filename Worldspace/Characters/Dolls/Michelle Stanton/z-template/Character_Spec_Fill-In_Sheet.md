# Character Spec — Fill-In Sheet

**What this is:** a conversational intake document for developing a character's fundamental, inherent,
intrinsic qualities — the "who's involved" layer this repo exists to hold, alongside the timeline ("when")
and worldspace ("where") layers. It's organized as a set of questions with a short rationale under each — fill
in whatever you have a clear sense of under "Your answer," and leave the rest as `TBD`. Nothing here needs to
be answered all at once, and nothing here needs to be answered in order. Ported over from InnerTepeniaGDD's
own `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/z-template/Character_Spec_Fill-In_Sheet.md` (2026-08-06), rebuilt around this
repo's own existing field set rather than that project's — see "What was deliberately left out" at the bottom.

**How this maps onto the existing structure:** this sheet's sections feed directly into the standard
character folder skeleton (`README.md`, `Personal_Background/Formative_History.md`,
`Personal_Background/Relationships.md`, `Personal_Background/Loyalties.md`, `Personal_Background/Timeline.md`,
`Personal_Background/Conflict_Experiences.md`). You don't need to write in that structured format yourself;
write in plain prose here, in your own words, and the structured files get built from your answers afterward.

**Where to put it:** copy this file into a character's own folder (alongside their `README.md`) once you're
ready to start developing them. Applies to both Dolls and humans — `Formative_History.md` and
`Conflict_Experiences.md` already carry their own dual framing for each.

**If you don't have a direct answer to a section, here's what to give me instead.** None of the sections below
require you to already have a finished answer. If a section stalls you out, any of the following raw material
works just as well, and I'll do the derivation:

- **A comparison.** A real person you know, an existing fictional character, or another character already in
  this universe — "she's like X, but Y." A comparison plus one point of difference is usually enough for me to
  pattern-match the rest.

  TBD

- **A single scene or image, however small.** Not a plot — just one moment you can picture them in: something
  they say, something they're doing, how they react to one specific thing. Often enough to reverse-engineer a
  much larger answer from.

  TBD

- **What you don't want.** Ruling something out ("I don't want him to be a generic stoic soldier," "I don't
  want her weakness to be that she's 'too nice'") narrows the space just as effectively as picking something.

  TBD

- **Tone/vibe words.** A handful of adjectives for how they should feel overall — wry, quietly tragic, warm,
  unsettling — even before any specifics exist.

  TBD

- **Whatever's already written about them**, even fragments — an existing `README.md` stub, their
  `Reference_Images/` folder, a note in a project-specific repo that hasn't propagated here yet. I can mine
  existing material directly rather than making you restate it.

  TBD

## 4. Personality, in Your Own Words

Don't worry about Enneagram terminology or any other framework — just describe them the way you'd describe
someone you know: what they're like, what they care about, what irritates them, what they avoid, how they'd
react under pressure. Adjectives, a comparison to another character, a specific behavior you picture them
doing — anything concrete helps more than an abstract label. I'll take what you give me here and propose a
matching Enneagram type/subvariant.

While you're in here, four more specific things worth including if you have a sense of them:
- **Their fundamental flaw or weakness** — not a cute quirk, an actual liability that can cause real problems.
- **What they're genuinely good at** — skills or strengths, practical or otherwise.
- **What they value most** — the people, ideals, or things they care about above everything else.
- **Any internal contradiction** — two things they want that can't both be true at once (Maggie Aarden's own
  SP 5w6 pull between wanting to withdraw and being unable to abandon people who need her is the reference
  example for what this looks like — see her own folder in `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/`).

## 14. Character Craft Notes

A few reflective, meta-level questions — useful for keeping the overall cast varied and intentional, not
required for the character to function:
- **Inspiration mix** — roughly how much comes from a real person you know, pure imagination, a famous figure
  or literary character, versus just intuitive "this feels right"? A rough split is fine.
- **Round or flat, on purpose** — multidimensional and evolving, or functional and single-note by design? Flat
  isn't a flaw here — it can be the right call for a background, comic-relief, or pure world-building role.
- **Anti-stereotype check** — is this character deliberately subverting a stereotype some part of their design
  might otherwise invite? Worth flagging if a given nationality/origin needs a deliberate counter-note.

## What Was Deliberately Left Out

Ported from InnerTepeniaGDD's own version of this sheet, with the following removed or adapted since they're
specific to that project's own systems rather than this repo's cross-project "who's involved" layer:

- **MACHINE stat profile, Companion Potential, Personal Questline Hook.** Game-specific stat blocks and
  romance/companion flags belong in that project's own repo, per this template's own `README.md` — not
  duplicated here.
- **The three-way category split** (Still-Present / Past-History / Off-World-or-MIA) InnerTepeniaGDD organizes
  its own Dolls by. This repo's `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/` folder is flat, not split that way, so Section
  12 above covers narrative role in general terms instead.
- **"How loyalties may shift based on player actions"** (Section 11's InnerTepeniaGDD equivalent) — a
  game-mechanic concept with no equivalent here.
- **Section/file renames to match this repo's own existing, already-generalized field names:**
  `PreWar_History.md` → `Formative_History.md` (broadened to cover both Dolls and humans, not just
  pre-war-framed), `War_Experiences.md` → `Conflict_Experiences.md` (broadened beyond just the wars Inner
  Tepenia's own timeline sits closest to).

## Notes
