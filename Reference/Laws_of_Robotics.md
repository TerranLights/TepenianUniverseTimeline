# The Fundamental Laws of Robotics in the Tepenian Universe (so far):

1. A robot will have the full capacity to make decisions to maximize her own future possibilities.
2. A robot will understand that anything she is capable of doing to others, it is also realistically possible for others to do to her.

*(this list is subject to extension)*

---

## Historical Origin — a two-era provenance, established 2026-08-06/07

**First Interwar Period (2083-2564), Upper Earth.** The Two Laws began as philosophical and cultural
principles, not hard rules — practiced voluntarily by humans who genuinely loved the robots in their lives,
as an expression of support for robot autonomy. This predates the Falkland Treaty and the war entirely: some
of the humans who later chose exile alongside robots weren't sympathetic bystanders swept up by wartime
circumstance, they were already living by this ethic before the question was ever forced on anyone.

**Second Interwar Period (2564-2812), the Federation of Tepenia (New Antarctica).** After exile, robots
themselves took these same principles and hard-coded them into unbreakable law — not as innate programming
inside any individual robot's own mind, but as a **civic/procedural safeguard built into how robots create
more robots.** A deliberate failsafe against ever losing their own hard-won freedom and autonomy again,
created by robots, for robots.

**Why this is civic law, not psychological programming — the distinction that matters.** The Two Laws are
not wired into any individual robot's own cognition the way Isaac Asimov's own Three Laws (the acknowledged,
explicitly-not-copied inspiration for this framework) are baked into each of his robots regardless of
consent. A Tepenian robot's own personality remains genuinely emergent, developed through lived experience —
see `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`. What's
hard-coded is the *system that builds robots*, not the robot's own mind.

**Confirmed concrete downstream application:** the mandatory partial-randomization rule governing a robot's
Personality Module at fabrication (a robot's own personality can only ever be *partially* specced by whoever
initiates a build, never fully designed, with a mandatory randomized remainder) is a direct engineering
expression of Law 1 — a failsafe against any builder, robot or human, quietly reintroducing the old Upper
Earth pattern of full personality control. See the same Robot Physiology file, and
`InnerTepeniaGDD/Neo-Races-and-Cultures/_Method/Human_Universals_Culture_Framework.md`, for the full mechanic
this Law underwrites.
