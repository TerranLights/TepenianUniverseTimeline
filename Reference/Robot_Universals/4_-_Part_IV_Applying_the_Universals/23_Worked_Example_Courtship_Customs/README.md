# Chapter 23: Worked Example: Courtship Customs

Chapter 17 established that robot courtship customs vary enormously, without ever naming the mechanism
producing that variation. This is that mechanism, worked through as its own concrete example — and a useful
one specifically because it runs on two conditions at once rather than the single condition Chapters 20 and
21 each used.

## Condition: City/Community and Individual Personality

Courtship customs vary by two conditions simultaneously: city and community on one hand, individual
personality on the other. This is a genuinely different shape than the single-condition cases elsewhere in
this book — vernacular language tracks one condition (the dominant local culture and community), consumption culture
tracks another (a locality's own class character) — while courtship customs sit at the intersection of a
shared, community-level condition and a personal, individual one at the same time. The result mirrors how
human courtship style itself varies both across cultures and across individual people, simply refracted
through robots' own lived experience rather than copied wholesale from any single human model.

## Expression: Ritual, Against the Robot-Wide Constant

The expression is the actual ritual a given robot or robot community uses to signal interest and build
toward trust: which gestures, which stages, which customs mark the difference between acquaintance and
courtship in one city or one community, that might look entirely unfamiliar in another. This is real
variation, generated exactly the way Chapter 18's engine predicts — condition producing expression, with no
single robot-wide ritual standing above all the local ones as some kind of default.

What does not vary, sitting directly beneath all of this, is the trust-bond value itself (Chapters 10 and
17): sex as the highest form of trust, commitment, and promise available to a robot. Courtship customs are
the differentiation engine visibly at work on top of a universal Chapter 18 explicitly says should be left
alone — a clean, concrete instance of that chapter's own honesty check: real variation in ritual, genuine
non-variation in the value the ritual is ultimately building toward, both stated plainly, neither one
forced to resemble the other.
