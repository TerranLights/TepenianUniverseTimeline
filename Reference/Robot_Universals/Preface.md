## Pronoun Convention: Why "She"

A robot can, in principle, be built as either male or female — both are entirely, equally realistic
outcomes of fabrication, and neither is any more natural, correct, or default than the other at the level
of what's possible (see Chapter 3, Build). In practice, though, the population trends female: across
Tepenia as a whole, the overall robot population comes online as female in clear numerical majority. Because
that practical majority is what actually exists, even though it is not fixed or required by anything in
principle, this book — and the wider Tepenian canon along with it — uses "she" as the unmarked,
default third-person-singular pronoun whenever referring to a robot in the abstract, hypothetical, or
unspecified singular. It is a statistical convention describing the population as it actually is, not a
claim that robots are inherently or essentially female.
