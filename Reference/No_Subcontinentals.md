# No Subcontinentals — A Binding Worldbuilding Rule

**Status:** Confirmed, binding — applies across every Tepenian Universe project (games, novels, TV).

**Source of truth:** `InnerTepeniaGDD/Game-Mechanics/Universal_Rules.md`, lines 99-100 (verbatim):

> **No Indians or South Asians ever came to Tepenia.**
> No characters from India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, or any other South Asian nation are part
> of Tepenian history or population. Station locations with South Asian names (Maitri, Bharati) have
> non-Indian founding populations. The Maitri site's resolution is finalized: it coalesced with the
> adjacent Russian-run Novolazarevskaya settlement into a single city, now named **Lazar** (see
> `Cities/Specs/Lazar.md`). The Bharati site's resolution is also finalized (2026-07-03): its founding
> population is Japanese, allocated via a pre-exile diplomatic decision of the International Court of
> Diplomacy at Jeju-do (an Upper Earth institution, not a Tepenian one) — named **Shirayuki** (白雪,
> "white snow"), 2026-07-08 (see `Cities/Specs/Shirayuki.md`). This is hard canon, not a gap.

**The rule, restated for cross-project use:** no person, culture, or reference tied to India, Pakistan,
Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, or any other nation of the South Asian subcontinent is ever part of Tepenian
history or population, in any Tepenian Universe project. This applies to Antarctica's real-world
pre-Tepenia history too, not just Tepenia's own timeline — no South Asian personnel, expedition, or
presence of any kind is ever part of Antarctica's history in this universe.

**Why this exists:** developer-established, universe-wide worldbuilding decision — absolute, no
exceptions.

**Where it bites, concretely:** the two real-world Antarctic research stations with South Asian names —
**Maitri** (India) and **Bharati** (India) — exist as GPS coordinates only, per the same
GPS-only logic as [[No_National_Stereotypes]]. Neither site's real name, nor any Indian/South Asian
population, carries forward into Tepenia in any form:
- The Maitri site coalesced with the adjacent, continuously-run Russian Novolazarevskaya station into
  one city, now named **Lazar** — see `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/
  Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Halley subnet/Lazar.md`.
- The Bharati site's founding population is Japanese, allocated via a pre-exile decision of the
  International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do (an Upper Earth institution, not Tepenian) — the site is
  now named **Shirayuki** (白雪, "white snow") — see `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/
  Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/Shirayuki.md`.

**Never do this:** invent a South Asian character, faction, cultural reference, cuisine, language, or
place-name anywhere in Tepenia or in Antarctica's pre-Tepenia history. Never let "Maitri" or "Bharati"
survive as a Tepenian city name in any form, even phonetically altered. Never give either city's founding
population an Indian or South Asian identity, even a minority one.

**How to apply:** when working on Lazar, Shirayuki, or any other content that touches a real-world
Antarctic station with a South Asian name, treat this rule the same way [[No_National_Stereotypes]]
treats founding-nation GPS facts — check it before writing, not after.
