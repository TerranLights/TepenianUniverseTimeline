# Robot Universals — Summary

**The full book now lives in `DerivationMechanisms`** (a cross-project repo of derivation mechanisms, not
canon data): `Characters/Robot-specific/from TepenianUniverseTimeline/Robot_Universals/`. This file is a
one-page summary and pointer, kept here because `Repo_Scope.md` limits this repo to *when/where/who* — a
generic differentiation methodology is a "how," and belongs in `DerivationMechanisms` instead.

**What it is:** the central, cross-project reference for robot meta-culture across the entire Tepenian
multimedia universe — Inner Tepenia, the Cryptograph Helix novels, the "Southern Lights" TV continuity, the
Outer Tepenia series, and anything set in this universe going forward. Any robot population, in any
Tepenia-branded work, should be writable as a specific variation on the floor this book establishes, never
a contradiction of it.

**Method:** borrows Donald E. Brown's *Human Universals* (McGraw-Hill, 1991) methodology — the
essence/accident distinction, and the Direct-Transfer / New-Universal / Non-Transfer classification scheme
— applied to an original robot culture, not an adaptation of Brown's own content.

**Pronoun convention (Preface):** robots trend female in population numbers, though male is an equally
realistic fabrication outcome. "She" is used as the unmarked default pronoun for a robot in the abstract —
a statistical convention, not an essentialist claim.

**What it deliberately does not contain:** any specific population's actual culture (a particular Concordia
district, a particular city, a particular colony). That stays in each project's own repo. This is the floor
and the method, not the catalog.

## Structure (four parts, 27 chapters)

- **Part I — Ground-Level Fundamentals** *(the technical/biological/legal substrate before culture forms)*:
  Fundamental Laws of Robotics · The Gel Brain · Build · Mortality & Integrity · Personality · Language.
- **Part II — Differentiation Method** *(essence-vs-accident: where robot experience necessarily departs
  from human)*: The Cognitive Imperative · Reproduction/Kinship and the Absence of Genetic Relatedness ·
  Substrate and Age · Sexual Jealousy.
- **Part III — Robot Cultural Universals** *(the positive content, topical)*: Kinship/Clan/Mentor-Mentee ·
  Cooperation/Morality/Emergent Social Convention · City-Locality as the Seat of Identity · Generational
  Identity (Gen/Mark) · Religion and Belief · Consumption and Vice Culture · Sexuality.
- **Part IV — Applying the Universals** *(the method formalized, plus worked examples already in canon)*:
  The Differentiation Engine · Weighing the Axes · worked examples (Vernacular Language, Consumption
  Culture, Religious Traditions, Courtship Customs, Arcanet Etiquette, Calethina's Split Brain, Sumerian's
  Liturgical Register) · Cross-Media Application.

**For the full text of any chapter, read it in `DerivationMechanisms`** — this summary does not duplicate
chapter content.
