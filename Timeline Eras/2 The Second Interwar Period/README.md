# The Second Interwar Period — **2564–2812**

**The era between the Falkland Treaty and the Long Night War.** *248 years.* **The default frame for
worldbuilding a Tepenian location** *(before the war, before Amundsen Tower's destruction)* — see each
project's own location methodology for how that default is applied.

📎 **Chronology: `Timeline.md`** *(this folder)*.

---

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE GRAND-TIMELINE ACTS — **the era is NOT culturally uniform, and this is the single most important thing to know about it**

**Developer rulings, 2026-09-06.** ⛔ **Binding across every Tepenian Universe project.**
⭐ **Full statement, with the sequencing and divergence rules it belongs to:
`Reference/No_National_Stereotypes.md`.**

| Act | Roughly | What the people ARE |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **ACT 1** | **2564 → early 2600s** *(~40–50 years)* | ***"Chinese / Japanese / American / Russian / Mexican **who live in Antarctica**."*** **National-ethnic-origin cultures are still FRESH for them** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **ACT 2** | **~late 2600s / early 2700s onward** | ## ***Properly TEPENIAN — in both name and cultural identity.*** **Origin is now ANCESTRY, not identity** |

> ***"…similar to the way that by the early-1800s, a person from Pennsylvania and a person from Virginia may
> very well have very different ethnic backgrounds and local cultures — they were both still fundamentally
> AMERICAN."***

> ## ⚠⚠ **THE ERA SPANS BOTH ACTS AND IS MOSTLY ACT 2.**
> **Act 1 is roughly the first `18%` of the period.** ***So a story or a location pass that writes its
> characters as their founding nation is writing the wrong Act for about 200 of these 248 years.***

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE MECHANISM — **interconnection, decisively solidified by Amundsen Tower's completion (~2688)**

> ***"That 'metacultural unification' was ONE OF (among multitudinous other) effects of the completion of
> Amundsen Tower. Their being fully interconnected further defined their sense of national identity in who
> they are as a nation of people (both robots and humans alike)."***

## ⛔ FOUR QUALIFICATIONS THAT MUST NOT BE DROPPED

| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | ⛔ **NOT a switch.** *The unification was already **in progress** before construction of the Tower even began* |
| **2** | ⛔⛔ **NOT the creation of unity.** ***Before the Tower, the people already had a sense of shared struggles and shared goals in a shared country. They did NOT consider themselves "psychologically / culturally / spiritually separate" from each other*** |
| **3** | ⛔ **NOT the Tower's only effect** — *"one of, among multitudinous other."* **The Tower is primarily energy regulation. It is not the cause of everything** |
| **4** | ⭐ **What it DID:** *full interconnection, which **further defined** and was **"the decisive force that helped solidify their sense of national identity."*** |

> ### ⭐⭐ **AN ACT BOUNDARY IS A CONSOLIDATION, NOT A LINE CROSSED.** *Write the transition as a gradient with a decisive moment inside it.*

## ⚠⚠ AND `~2688` IS CROWDED — **check what else sits at your date**

**Amundsen Tower's completion · the opening of the ORBITAL TIER · the Census I → Census II boundary** *(which
the census records as **pre-war migration to orbit, not loss**)* **· and, regionally, the Tri-Cities' de facto
amalgamation.**

> ⭐⭐⭐ ***So the same structure whose completion solidified a shared national identity is also the structure
> through which people left.*** **That is national context, true everywhere — therefore never a
> differentiator between locations.** ⭐ **What differentiates is how hard a given place used that exit.**

# ⚠ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR ANY PASS, STORY OR SCENE SET IN THIS ERA

1. ⛔ **Place your events against the Act boundary.** ***The same event in Act 1 and in Act 2 happens to
   different peoples.***
2. ⭐ **Correct form of words:** ⛔ *"a Japanese city"* → ✅ ***"a Japan-FOUNDED Tepenian city."***
   **Stock: Japanese. People: Tepenian. Local culture: its own.**
3. ⭐⭐ **Differentiate LOCALLY, converge NATIONALLY.** *Local cultures stay genuinely different;* ⛔ **national
   identity is not a differentiator — every Tepenian is Tepenian.**
4. **Composition names the STOCK; time and place produce the CULTURE.** *Operator: time · separation · local
   environmental setting · local struggles and hardships · local goals · local sensibilities and habits.*
